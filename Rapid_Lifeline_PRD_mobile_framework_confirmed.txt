Rapid Lifeline — PRD v1.1 (Final amendment to v1.0)
10 Nov 2026 · @Iam Srm
Rapid Lifeline gets a patient from one booking tap to the right hospital's emergency door, with the family watching live. Every decision below is settled; nothing is left open for the coding agent.
1. Overview and settled decisions
Rapid Lifeline is an on-demand ambulance booking product for Bengaluru, launching with English and Kannada, where a patient or bystander books in 3 taps, is matched to the right hospital, and the family tracks the trip live. The receiving hospital is a first-class user and is alerted before the patient arrives.

Hospital coordination starts in parallel with ambulance dispatch and must never delay driver assignment. The initial hospital alert enters pending_response. At 10 s without a response, send an urgent reminder to the hospital and alert operations. At 20 s, transition that alert to timed_out (never accepted), alert operations, write timeout and rerouting events to the audit trail, and immediately search for the next suitable hospital. 5. Feature list
The customer booking journey is C1 Home → C2 Ambulance Selection → C4 Confirmation → C5 Finding Ambulance → C6 Live Tracking → C7 Completion. C2 Continue (tap 2) navigates directly to C4 with the recommended hospital preselected. C4 shows the selected category, hospital, fare estimate, pickup and destination; Change hospital opens C3, and selecting a hospital in C3 returns to C4 with that choice. Confirm and book is the final booking action (tap 3). On confirmation, ambulance dispatch and hospital coordination start in parallel; hospital acceptance must never gate or delay driver matching. The hospital sees its inbound alert and, after assignment, the current driver/ETA as available. The driver accepts the dispatch offer, navigates to pickup, and after pickup follows the driver-confirmed hospital destination; family tracking and the remaining trip milestones proceed as specified.

Final decision
Fare estimate
Range = estimate to estimate × 1.15, where estimate = base + max(0, km − 5) × per-km, km = pickup to hospital by road; night surcharge 20% from 22:00 to 06:00 IST applies to the whole fare
Final fare
Computed from actual driven distance and waiting time; capped at 125% of the range top unless the rider changed the hospital
Platform fee
12% of the fare, deducted from the operator payout; cash trips settle weekly

Offer timeout
Each driver has 20 s to accept; up to 5 sequential offers; then status no_driver

Cancellation
Free within 60 s of confirming or before assignment; after assignment ₹100; if the driver is within 500 m of pickup ₹250; no fee if the driver cancels or is more than 10 minutes late
Guest booking
Phone number only; OTP is verified during the trip; max 3 guest bookings per phone per day
Hospital coordination
Starts in parallel with driver dispatch; hospital acceptance never delays driver assignment. Each alert is pending_response until explicit acceptance, decline, redirect or timeout. At 10 s without a response, send an urgent hospital reminder and alert operations. At 20 s, mark the alert timed_out (never accepted), alert operations, record timeout and rerouting events in the audit trail, and immediately search for the next suitable hospital. A decline triggers the same next-hospital search. Any redirected or newly proposed destination remains pending until the assigned driver confirms it; on rejection or no suitable alternative, alert operations and leave the destination unchanged pending resolution. Silence is never acceptance. Record booking.confirmed, dispatch.search_started, dispatch.assigned, dispatch.no_driver, hospital.alerted, hospital.reminder_sent, hospital.accepted, hospital.declined, hospital.redirected, hospital.timed_out, hospital.alternative_search_started, hospital.alternative_found, hospital.destination_change_proposed, hospital.destination_change_confirmed, hospital.destination_change_rejected and hospital.needs_ops_resolution as applicable.
